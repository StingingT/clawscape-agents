`README.md` in `C:\Users\guyro\Downloads`.

The supplied package describes itself as a **partial** RuneScape 2004 item
knowledge catalog, not an exhaustive all-items database. Its target deliverable
is a versioned database containing every authoritative item and every supported
acquisition route, with unresolved cases kept visible.

The worked examples used by the bootstrap are:

- Iron dagger `1203`: ground record at `3248, 3245, 0`.
- Bronze dagger `1205`: ground record at `3213, 3218, 1`.
- Bronze pickaxe `1265`: ground records at `3229, 3215, 0` and
  `3229, 3223, 0`, plus an unresolved `axeshop` stock candidate.
- Raw chicken `2138`: one-item grant in the chicken death script; NPC binding
  and spawn remain unresolved.
- Feather `314`: chicken death-script branches granting 15 or 5; probability

The supplied README warns that source coordinates do not prove reachability,
map loading or current server availability, and that a stock row does not prove
a shopkeeper, price or access route. Those constraints are represented in the
machine-readable records and query library.
