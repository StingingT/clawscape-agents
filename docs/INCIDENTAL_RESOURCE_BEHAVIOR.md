# Incidental resource behavior

Agents keep their Director-selected primary objective while evaluating cheap secondary opportunities. Fresh reachable ground resources may become bounded support work when carrying them has plausible future value and sufficient inventory capacity. Collected incidental resources may be stored for later processing, supplies, collection, crafting or another independently justified goal.

Low-cost item actions that directly create progression may be used in place when observed, but they do not create a new primary objective and remain subject to normal build/protected-XP dispatch guards. Opportunism must yield to combat safety, active dependencies, inventory efficiency, route cost and unresolved actions.

This policy is intentionally generic: it does not depend on a named creature or named drop.
