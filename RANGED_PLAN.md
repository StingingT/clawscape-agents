# Stinger ammunition plan

Stinger keeps at least 15 arrows while training and buys back up to 50 when
possible. At level 1 it uses bronze or iron arrows; at level 5 it prefers steel.
Later upgrades are only adopted when they appear in a live shop, bank, drop, or
fletching interface: mithril at 20 Ranged, adamant at 30, and rune at 40.

The world data confirms that Lowe's Archery Emporium in eastern Varrock stocks
bronze, iron, and steel arrows. Stinger travels there only when low on arrows,
opens the shop through Lowe, reads the live stock and price, and buys the best
permitted arrow it can afford. With no coins at the emergency threshold, it
uses nearby pickpocket opportunities to fund the resupply rather than trying to
fight with an empty quiver.
